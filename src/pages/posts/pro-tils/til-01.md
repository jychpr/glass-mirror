I read a paper about DINO. Another party sent me a paper about DINO.
And it's not the same DINO.

One is DINO for DETR, with the paper name 'DINO: DETR with Improved DeNoising Anchor Boxes for End-to-End Object Detection'.

The other paper is 'Emerging Properties in Self-Supervised Vision Transformers' in which their proposed method named DINO.

So, make sure to check the previous papers and check the references.
